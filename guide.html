The screens here come from Doppelmaker's digital twin: Studio running for real, with two simulated phones and a simulated person. Your room and your phones will look different; the steps are the same.

The Studio window

Studio opens on Home, with nothing to fill in. One window holds everything:

  • Sidebar: Home, Library, Rig, and your scans, grouped by subject.
  • Toolbar: the page's title and New Scan… (⌘N). Settings is ⌘, or the button at the foot of the sidebar.
  • Status bar: both phones, the calibration, the empty room, and Pair a phone…. Click any of them to go to the Rig page.

What you can do from where

Every place in Studio, the things you might want there, and where each one takes you. A scan is the document: its recordings, shape, cuts and print settings stay together, and its stages run left to right.

Pair the phones

Once per phone. Pair a phone… shows a QR code. Point the iPhone's Camera at it and tap the banner, or in Doppelmaker choose Settings › This Mac › Pair… › Scan the code on the Mac. That's the whole pairing: there is nothing to type. The code works for one phone, once, for five minutes, and it carries your Mac's certificate, so the phone talks only to your Mac, encrypted.

Then put the phone on its stand. A paired phone is always a rig phone: Studio controls it from then on, with roles, recording settings (Settings › Phones on the Mac), and starting and stopping every take. You shouldn't need to touch the phones again for the rest of the day. Recordings the Mac has are deleted from the phone after a few days by themselves.

Set up the rig

The Rig page shows four live views: each phone's colour picture and its LiDAR depth, coloured by distance, with anything outside the subject's zone dimmed. Check the subject is inside both phones' depth from neck to hips. The page warns when the top of the subject leaves a phone's view. It is set up once and stays set up until a phone moves. Steps along the top: Phones, Calibrate, Empty room, Ready. The space bar presses each step's main button.

Calibrate: hold the castle where the subject's chest will be, in view of both phones, and press Take snapshot. Move it somewhere new and take another, about six to eight in all. Each live view draws the castle's frame where the phone thinks it is, a 3 × 3 grid of where the castle has been, and a yellow next: here box.

Empty room: nobody in either picture, lights as they'll be for the scan, then Record empty room (3 s). It's the background the subject's outline is cut against, worth 3–4 mm of girth, and it's kept with the calibration.

Start a scan

New Scan… asks three things: what you're scanning (a person's torso, a person's head, or the test target); and who, which is optional and can be named later or never. Every scan is recorded on the rig; for a head, the phones go up to the head and the subject sits on a stool on the turntable. Studio never asks for gender: whether there is a bust to measure is read from the shape, and Scan info can change it.

Capture

A scan opens on its first stage, Capture. On the rig: Subject, Scan, Arms up (optional), Finish. If the rig isn't ready, or a phone has moved since calibration, the card says what's missing and takes you to the Rig page.

Press Start scan and turn the turntable steadily. The card shows how far the subject has turned, measured live from the phones' depth rather than estimated from a timer. Stop once it passes the target, 750° by default: two full turns plus some overlap.

Check the result

The stages along the top light up as they finish. Scan check gives a verdict while the subject is still there, so you can scan again on the spot. Outline diagnostics opens inside the window, with a way back.

Export makes a Bambu Studio project, the STL files, or a scan archive (a .zip). Exporting print files locks the scan for printing: nothing about its shape changes until you choose Re-open for changes, so a later Studio never alters a form you have printed.

Come back to a scan later: the Library

The Library shows every scan you have made, grouped by subject, with a picture of the shape, a frame from the recording, the main measurements, and whether it was printed. Find a subject by name, or show only torsos, heads or printed scans.

  • Load for printing opens the scan's print plan just as it was, to print another copy or export again.
  • Re-open for changes opens it to edit; a scan locked for printing asks first.
  • The ⋯ menu exports a scan archive, compacts a scan (keeps what printing needs, drops results that can be recomputed), locks or unlocks it, or deletes it.
  • Storage, at the top, shows what the data folder holds and how much space the Mac has left. Clear system data empties the Trash and removes unfinished uploads, old logs and picture caches. Below it, choose how long deleted scans, unfinished uploads and unused phones are kept, and whether old scans are compacted by themselves.
  • Every scan also stays in the sidebar. Open it and everything is as you left it, edits included.
  • Scan info sets the title and the subject, overrides the chest, and offers Reprocess, which runs every stage again with the current Studio and keeps your recordings and edits.
  • Open a scan from a file (Home, or File menu) brings back a scan archive (.zip) or a scan folder from another Mac. Recordings and edits come with it, and the rest is recomputed.

Check the rig's accuracy

Scan the printed test target like a person: New Scan › The test target. Instead of a form, the last stage is Accuracy. Studio aligns the scan with the target's exact shape (turning and moving it, never scaling) and reports, section by section, how far the surface is from the truth, the girth errors a tape would see, and whether black parts read differently from white ones.

Messages

Problems appear in a Needs attention box under the step card, each with what to do.

MessageWhat to do
Phone A is offline.Unlock it and open Doppelmaker. Same Wi-Fi as the Mac.
This Mac has 3.1 GB free; a take needs about 9 GB.Free some space: Library › Storage › Clear system data, or compact old scans.
This scan was exported for printing and is locked.Re-open it for changes (the banner above the stages, or the Library) if you mean to change it.
Phone B is hot.Take it out of its case, keep it on its charger, and give it a few minutes.
Phone A is at 15%.Plug it in. LiDAR recording drains the battery quickly.
Snapshot s04 disagrees with the others.The castle probably moved during that snapshot. Open Calibration details and press Drop next to it.
No empty-room recording for this calibration.Record it on the Rig page before the subject steps on.
Phone A moved since calibration.Its picture shifted against the room. Tighten its stand, then calibrate again on the Rig page.
