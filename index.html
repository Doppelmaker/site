How it works

From a two-minute scan to parts on the print bed

The pictures below come from Doppelmaker's digital twin: a simulated person, simulated phones, and the real software processing what they record. Real-world results come with the first beta.

Scan

She turns slowly on a turntable while two fixed iPhones record camera, LiDAR depth and motion. Doppelmaker finds her outline in every frame, even with people or pets moving behind her.

Shape

LiDAR and outlines from both phones are fused into one surface, with her sway and breathing taken out. Landmarks and tape measurements are found automatically.

Design

Set the neckline, shoulders, arm pieces and hem in Studio. Doppelmaker builds a rigid core inside a soft shell that takes pins, with keys so the pieces only go together one way.

Print

The form is cut where it prints best for your printer: as few pieces as fit the bed, oriented to need almost no supports, exported straight to Bambu Studio.

The same form laid out for a Bambu Lab H3C: five plates, each piece turned to print with almost no support.

Accuracy

Millimetres, not sizes

A dress form is only useful if it measures like the person. On the digital twin, with LiDAR errors that don't average away and a subject who leans and breathes, Doppelmaker's tape measurements land within a couple of millimetres:

+0.7 mmbust girth
−0.2 mmunderbust girth
+1.8 mmwaist girth
0.4 mmmedian surface error

Simulated results; LiDAR alone was 11–13 mm short on the same scan. Real-world numbers will be published as they're measured.

The rig

Two phones, a turntable and a printed target

The phones stay put; the person turns. Fixed phones never lose track of the room, so Doppelmaker can follow her own small movements (a lean, a breath) and take them out, which a hand-held scan can't.

Before each session, a printed calibration castle tells Doppelmaker exactly where the phones are. A larger test target, a shape of precisely known size, checks the whole chain end to end.

Also

Wig heads

The same pipeline scans a head and neck and finds the hairline and any marked lines in the photos, then engraves them into the printed head as fine grooves, so a wig maker's block matches the wearer exactly.

Doppelmaker Scanner

For iPhone (Pro models with LiDAR, iOS 17+). Records the scan and sends it to your Mac over your own Wi-Fi.

Doppelmaker Studio

For Mac with Apple silicon. Builds the shape, lets you adjust landmarks and cuts, and exports print-ready files.

Private by design

No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. Scans go from your phone to your Mac and nowhere else. Privacy policy.

Doppelmaker is in active development and not yet on the App Store. A TestFlight beta for a small group comes first. Questions: support@doppelmaker.app.
