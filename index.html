A dress form shaped exactly like you.

Doppelmaker turns an iPhone scan into a custom dress form or wig head that you print yourself: a rigid core under a soft, pinnable shell, split into pieces that fit your printer.

Everything stays on your own devices.

Two apps, one workshop

Doppelmaker Scanner

For iPhone. Records the camera, LiDAR depth and motion while the person turns, and sends each recording to your Mac over your own Wi-Fi.

Doppelmaker Studio

For Mac. Builds the 3D shape, finds the landmarks and cut lines, lets you adjust them, and exports print-ready files for your slicer.

How it works

  1. Set up the rig. Two iPhones on stands, the person on a turntable, and a printed calibration target.
  2. Scan. One or two slow turns. The phones record; the person stands naturally.
  3. Check and adjust. Studio shows the fitted shape, the measurements and where the form will be cut: neck, shoulders, arms, hem.
  4. Print. Studio splits the form to fit your printer bed and exports the parts: a rigid core and a soft TPU shell you can pin into.

What you need

iPhones

Pro models with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or later), iOS 17 or later. Two phones for the rig.

A Mac

With Apple silicon. Studio does all the processing locally.

A 3D printer

Ideally one that prints two materials: a rigid core (PLA or PETG) and a flexible TPU shell.

Private by design

Body scans are personal. Doppelmaker has no accounts, no cloud processing and no analytics. Recordings go from the phone to your Mac on your local network and nowhere else. Details in the privacy policy.

Status

Doppelmaker is in active development and not yet available on the App Store. A TestFlight beta for a small group of testers comes first. Questions: support@doppelmaker.app.
