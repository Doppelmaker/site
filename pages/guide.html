The screens here come from Doppelmaker's digital twin: Studio running for real, with two simulated phones and a simulated person. Your room and your phones will look different; the steps are the same.

The Studio window

Studio opens on Home, with nothing to fill in. One window holds everything:

  • Sidebar: Home, Rig, and your scans, grouped by subject.
  • Toolbar: the page's title and New Scan… (⌘N). Settings is ⌘, or the button at the foot of the sidebar.
  • Status bar: both phones, the calibration, the empty room, and Pair a phone…. Click any of them to go to the Rig page.

What you can do from where

Every place in Studio, the things you might want there, and where each one takes you. A scan is the document: its recordings, shape, cuts and print settings stay together, and its stages run left to right.

Pair the phones

Once per phone. Pair a phone… shows a six-digit code and this Mac's address. On the iPhone, open Doppelmaker, go to Settings › This Mac, choose Pair and type the code. The phone finds the Mac on your Wi-Fi by itself.

For the rig, set each phone's Mode to Rig in its Settings and put it on its stand. From then on Studio controls it: roles, recording settings (Settings › Phones on the Mac), and starting and stopping every take. You shouldn't need to touch the phones again for the rest of the day.

Set up the rig

The Rig page is set up once and stays set up until a phone moves. Steps along the top: Phones, Calibrate, Empty room, Ready. The space bar presses each step's main button.

Calibrate: hold the castle where the subject's chest will be, in view of both phones, and press Take snapshot. Move it somewhere new and take another, about six to eight in all. Each live view draws the castle's frame where the phone thinks it is, a 3 × 3 grid of where the castle has been, and a yellow next: here box.

Empty room: nobody in either picture, lights as they'll be for the scan, then Record empty room (3 s). It's the background the subject's outline is cut against, worth 3–4 mm of girth, and it's kept with the calibration.

Start a scan

New Scan… asks three things: what you're scanning (a person's torso, a person's head, or the test target); who, which is optional and can be named later or never; and whether you're using the rig or one handheld phone. Studio never asks for gender: whether there is a bust to measure is read from the shape, and Scan info can change it.

Capture

A scan opens on its first stage, Capture. On the rig: Subject, Scan, Arms up (optional), Finish. If the rig isn't ready, or a phone has moved since calibration, the card says what's missing and takes you to the Rig page.

Press Start scan and turn the turntable steadily. The card shows how far the subject has turned, measured live from the phones' depth rather than estimated from a timer. Stop once it passes the target, 750° by default: two full turns plus some overlap.

Handheld: record with Doppelmaker Scanner (Scan torso or Scan head). The recording comes to the open scan by itself, or starts a new one if none is open.

Check the result

The stages along the top light up as they finish. Scan check gives a verdict while the subject is still there, so you can scan again on the spot. Outline diagnostics and Replay open inside the window, with a way back.

Export makes a Bambu Studio project, the STL files, or a scan archive.

Come back to a scan later

  • Every scan stays in the sidebar. Open it and everything is as you left it, edits included.
  • Scan info sets the title and the subject, overrides the chest, and offers Reprocess, which runs every stage again with the current Studio and keeps your recordings and edits.
  • Open a scan from a file (Home, or File menu) brings back a scan archive or a scan folder from another Mac. Recordings and edits come with it, and the rest is recomputed.

Check the rig's accuracy

Scan the printed test target like a person: New Scan › The test target. Instead of a form, the last stage is Accuracy. Studio aligns the scan with the target's exact shape (turning and moving it, never scaling) and reports, section by section, how far the surface is from the truth, the girth errors a tape would see, and whether black parts read differently from white ones.

Messages

Problems appear in a Needs attention box under the step card, each with what to do.

MessageWhat to do
Phone A is offline.Unlock it and open Doppelmaker (Mode: Rig). Same Wi-Fi as the Mac.
Phone B is hot.Take it out of its case, keep it on its charger, and give it a few minutes.
Phone A is at 15%.Plug it in. LiDAR recording drains the battery quickly.
Snapshot s04 disagrees with the others.The castle probably moved during that snapshot. Open Calibration details and press Drop next to it.
No empty-room recording for this calibration.Record it on the Rig page before the subject steps on.
Phone A moved since calibration.Its picture shifted against the room. Tighten its stand, then calibrate again on the Rig page.
