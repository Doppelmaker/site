Measures like a tape

Put a tape anywhere on the form and it reads within a couple of millimetres of the person in our simulations: the shape is checked against what both cameras see, not guessed from one.

Prints on your printer

Studio cuts the form into pieces that fit your bed, turned to need almost no supports, and opens them in Bambu Studio.

Stays on your devices

No accounts and no cloud. Scans go from the phone to your Mac over your own Wi-Fi, and nowhere else.

From scan to stand

About two minutes in front of the phones, a few minutes on the Mac, then the printer takes over.

  1. Scan

    The subject turns slowly on a turntable while two phones record. Doppelmaker traces their outline in every frame.

  2. Shape

    LiDAR depth and both outlines become one surface, with the subject's sway and breathing taken out.

  3. Design

    Choose the neckline, shoulders, arms and hem. Studio builds a rigid core inside a pinnable shell.

  4. Print

    Pieces fit your printer's bed and key together around a centre rod.

How it works, in more depth

Run it from a chair

The phones stay put and the subject turns, so you can sit at the Mac. Studio shows what both phones see, one step at a time: what to do, what to say to the subject, and a single button for it. It can even say the instructions out loud.

Walk through a whole session

A rigid core under a soft shell

The core is PLA or PETG and holds the shape. Around it, a TPU shell soft enough to take pins, printed together with the core on a two-material printer.

This one comes from our digital twin, a simulated person scanned by simulated phones and run through the real software. Open it, turn it, pull it apart.

Wig heads, too

The same scan of a head and neck becomes a wig block shaped like the wearer.

Draw the hairline on a cap before the scan. Doppelmaker finds the line in the photos and engraves it into the printed head as a fine groove, along with any other lines you mark, so the wig is built to the wearer's own hairline.

What you need

Two iPhone Pros

Any two Pro or Pro Max models from the iPhone 12 Pro on, on iOS 17; they don't need to match. Only the Pros have LiDAR, which measures depth directly. Why that matters

A Mac with Apple silicon

Doppelmaker Studio does all the processing on the Mac, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.
