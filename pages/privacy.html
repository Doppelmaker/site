Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

This policy covers Doppelmaker Scanner (iPhone), Doppelmaker Studio (Mac) and this website. In short: we do not collect your data. Your scans stay on devices you own.

What the apps record

Doppelmaker Scanner uses the iPhone's camera, LiDAR depth sensor and motion sensors to record a scan. Recordings are stored in the app's folder on the iPhone and sent to Doppelmaker Studio on a Mac you have paired, over your local network. Studio stores them, and everything it computes from them (3D shapes, measurements, print files), in a folder on that Mac.

Recordings are never sent to us or to any server. Neither app contains analytics, advertising or tracking code, and neither has accounts.

Permissions

  • Camera: to record images and LiDAR depth during a scan.
  • Motion: to record the phone's movement alongside the scan.
  • Local network: to find and send recordings to Studio on your Mac.

Backups

Files in the Scanner app's folder are included in your iPhone's backups if you have backups turned on (for example iCloud Backup). Studio's data folder is included in Mac backups such as Time Machine if that folder is backed up. Those backups are handled by Apple or your backup provider, not by us.

Beta testing

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides us with the information TestFlight shares with developers, such as your email address or name used for the invitation, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, and crash reports. We use this only to improve the apps. See Apple's TestFlight terms for details.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Children

Doppelmaker is not directed at children. Scanning a person requires their consent, or a parent's or guardian's for a minor.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@doppelmaker.app.
