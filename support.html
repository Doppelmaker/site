Support

Email support@doppelmaker.app. Doppelmaker is a small independent project, so replies may take a few days.

When reporting a problem, include the app (Scanner or Studio), its version, your iPhone or Mac model, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

Which iPhones work?

Scanner needs LiDAR, which only Pro iPhones have: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, on iOS 17 or later.

Do I need the Mac app?

Yes. Scanner records; Studio on your Mac turns the recordings into a shape and print files. The phone does not process scans on its own.

The phone can't find my Mac

  1. Make sure Doppelmaker Studio is running on the Mac and both are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the iPhone, check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and allow Doppelmaker.
  3. Some guest or office networks block devices from seeing each other. In Scanner's settings you can type the Mac's address, which Studio shows on its start screen.
  4. After an update, update both apps: older and newer versions may not find each other.

Pairing

Studio shows a 6-digit code; enter it in Scanner's settings. Pair again if you reinstall either app.

Where are my scans?

On the iPhone, in the Files app under On My iPhone › Doppelmaker, until they are sent to your Mac. On the Mac, in Studio's data folder (by default, DoppelmakerData in your home folder).

Deleting data

Delete recordings in Scanner, or the Doppelmaker folder in Files. On the Mac, delete a client in Studio or remove its folder from the data folder. There is no copy anywhere else.
